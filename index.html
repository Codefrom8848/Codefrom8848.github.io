Objective: Propose an integrated MIS for a medium-sized retail business operating across three branches in Nepal, addressing manual recordkeeping, disconnected spreadsheets, inventory tracking and delayed management reporting.

Technologies and methods: Database management concepts · SQL · PostgreSQL · System Analysis and Design · UML · Data Flow Diagrams

Outcome: Designed a multi-department network infrastructure with six VLANs, providing logical network separation and organised IP address allocation.
